Please add a new professional webpage that describes our CRM solution.

Context:
This CRM is part of the Nurbai/Mindsgate ecosystem. It is a practical, AI-assisted CRM designed for sales, relationship management, follow-ups, engagement tracking, and pipeline visibility. The CRM should feel modern, trustworthy, and useful for small to mid-sized businesses, MSPs, consultants, service businesses, and relationship-driven teams.

Goal:
Create a polished marketing/information page that explains what the CRM does, why it matters, and how it helps users stay on top of customer relationships.

Page purpose:
The page should explain the CRM solution in a clear, professional, non-hypey way. It should make the CRM feel like an intelligent command center for customer relationships, not just another contact database.

Suggested page title:
AI-Assisted CRM for Relationship-Driven Teams

Suggested subtitle:
Capture conversations, manage follow-ups, track engagements, and keep every opportunity moving forward from one intelligent workspace.

Page sections to include:

1. Hero section
   Create a strong hero area with:

* Main headline
* Short supporting paragraph
* Primary call-to-action button: “Explore the CRM”
* Secondary call-to-action button: “View Demo Workflow”

Suggested hero copy:
“Turn conversations into action.
Our CRM helps teams capture relationship activity, manage follow-ups, track engagements, and keep every customer opportunity moving without losing context.”

2. Problem section
   Explain the common problems businesses face:

* Follow-ups get missed
* Customer notes are scattered
* Sales activity is hard to track
* Teams rely on memory instead of process
* Opportunities stall because next actions are unclear

Keep the tone professional and practical.

3. Solution section
   Describe the CRM as a relationship operating system:

* Centralized company and contact records
* Engagement tracking
* Follow-up management
* Activity history
* Pipeline visibility
* AI-assisted voice/text capture
* Smart next-action prompts

4. Feature cards
   Create visually clean cards for these features:

Feature 1: Contact & Company Management
Description:
Keep people, companies, roles, decision-makers, communication preferences, and relationship history organized in one place.

Feature 2: Engagement Tracking
Description:
Track opportunities, relationship status, probability, next actions, and last-touch dates so nothing quietly disappears into the fog.

Feature 3: Follow-Up Command Center
Description:
See overdue, due today, and upcoming follow-ups in a focused dashboard designed to help users act quickly.

Feature 4: Activity Timeline
Description:
Automatically build a history of calls, emails, meetings, notes, and completed follow-ups.

Feature 5: AI Voice Capture
Description:
Use voice or natural language notes to create CRM actions, follow-ups, contacts, engagements, and activities faster.

Feature 6: Pipeline Visibility
Description:
View deals, leads, stages, and progress across the sales process with clear next steps.
